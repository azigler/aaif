# Interview-first drafting — ratified by Zig 2026-08-28 ("yes try 1 and these ideas")

The anti-slop finding that produced this: ban-lists remove tells but can't add a
person — v1.1 passed every mechanical gate and still read generic. The center of
the distribution moves only when the draft is MADE OF different material.

The process, for every long-form piece under his name:
1. INTERVIEW FIRST. Before any copy: Zig rambles on ~3 targeted questions (voice
   note transcript, sloppy typing, fragments — unedited). The draft is assembled
   FROM his sentences (quarried, not paraphrased-at). The draft becomes an edit
   of him, not a synthesis he edits.
2. ASYMMETRY PASS. Pick the one section that gets to be obsessive; starve the
   rest. Kill paragraphs that end on a neat cadence. Symmetry is the deepest tell.
3. UNSHARED KNOWLEDGE PER SECTION. Every section carries ≥1 thing from HIM or
   from a real measured artifact — never built purely from the outline.
4. ONE ROUGH EDGE stays: an unhedged opinion, a digression, a fragment. Sanding
   everything is the tell.

Status: proving on the pinki/promises piece first; promote into /zig-voice only
after it demonstrably cuts his edit volume (empirical before aspirational).
